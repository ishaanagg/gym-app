Kinetic Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Local-First Data Storage

Kinetic is a static, local-first web application. By default, all workout logs, progression targets, gym equipment configurations, and profile settings are stored exclusively on your own device inside your browser's IndexedDB storage. Kinetic does not operate any backend servers, analytics trackers, or user databases.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive in Settings, Kinetic requests the narrow https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission. This allows the app, running directly in your browser, to save and restore a single JSON backup file (kinetic-backup.json) inside your personal Google Drive's hidden appDataFolder.

  • Kinetic cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Your data travels directly between your browser and Google's APIs; the developer never receives, stores, or transmits your data.
  • OAuth access tokens are held only in browser memory during your active session and are never persisted or shared with third parties.

3. Data Deletion & Control

You can clear local data anytime via your browser settings, disconnect Google Drive inside Kinetic Settings, or delete Kinetic's hidden app data from your Google Drive settings under Settings → Manage Apps → Kinetic.
